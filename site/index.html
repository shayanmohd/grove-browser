Features

Room for everything you do on the web

Kamapathy runs on Chromium, so sites work the way you expect. Around them, it keeps the interface small and out of your way.

Spaces that stay apart

Work, personal and side projects each keep their own cookies and sign-ins. One shortcut lays every space out side by side.

Tabs across the top

One strip for the space you're in, with pinned pages and tab search for the days when it fills up.

Split view

Two live pages in one window. The address bar and controls follow whichever side you click.

Command menu

Press ⌘K to jump to any open tab or run any browser action without reaching for the mouse.

A library at hand

Bookmarks, history and downloads open as quick dialogs over the page, then get out of the way.

Quiet by design

No account, no feed, no telemetry. Light and dark themes with a single green accent that only shows up when it means something.

Agents

Your agent drives. You keep the keys.

Kamapathy gives coding and research agents a real browser without handing over yours. Each agent works in its own space, and you can see every step it takes.

  • A space of its own. Agent spaces have separate cookies and never see your tabs.
  • Take over anytime. One click pauses the agent. Let it continue when you're done.
  • You sign in, not the agent. It hands the space to you for passwords and codes, and password fields read as hidden in its snapshots.
  • Bring any agent. A small Node.js client and a ready-made skill work with Claude Code, Codex and anything else that can run a command.
  • Local and switchable. Agents connect over a private channel on your computer. One switch in Settings turns them all off.

Questions

      
Good to know

    

    

      

        Is Kamapathy free?
        
Yes. It's open source under the MIT license, and every build is free to download. The code is on GitHub.

      

      

        Does it come with an AI model?
        
No. Kamapathy is the browser your agent uses. Install the skill for Claude Code or Codex, or call the Node.js client from any agent that can run a command. There's no subscription and nothing to sign up for.

      

      

        Can an agent see my own tabs or accounts?
        
No. Agents only see the spaces they create, and those spaces have their own cookies and storage. When a site needs you to sign in, the agent hands the space to you and waits. You can take over or stop every agent at any time, and turning off agent access in Settings refuses all of them.

      

      

        Will my sites work?
        
Kamapathy uses Chromium, the engine behind Chrome and Edge, so pages render and behave the same way. Browser extensions, sync and a password manager aren't part of this preview yet.

      

      

        Why does my computer warn me when I open it?
        
The preview builds aren't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate yet, so macOS and Windows ask you to confirm the first launch. The steps above take a few seconds, and every release lists SHA-256 checksums you can check the download against.

      

      

        Does Kamapathy collect any data?
        
No. There's no account, no analytics and no telemetry. Your history, bookmarks and spaces stay on your computer.
